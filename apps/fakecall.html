About this app

FakeCall is a fun and practical virtual call simulator that lets you create realistic fake incoming calls on your Android device — in just a few MB. Whether you need a quick social excuse to escape an awkward meeting, want to prank your friends, or just enjoy a silly idol-themed morning call, FakeCall has you covered. ✨ Key Features • Ultra-Lightweight — The entire app is only a few MB. Install it in seconds, even on slow connections. • One-Tap Escape — Trigger a default fake call instantly with a single tap. No setup needed for quick emergencies. • Fully Customizable Caller — Set any contact name, phone number, location label, avatar, ringtone, and vibration pattern. Make it look like whoever you want. • Custom Ringtones & Wallpapers — Choose from system ringtones or import your own. Switch between multiple beautiful call screen themes to match different scenarios. • Scheduled Calls — Set a countdown timer (2s, 15s, 30s, or 1 minute) and let the call arrive exactly when you need it. • Realistic Call Screens — High-fidelity call interface with answer/reject buttons, call duration display, and smooth call-in transition. • Repeat Mode — Set the fake call to ring multiple times at custom intervals for extra authenticity. 🎯 Perfect For - Escaping awkward situations (boring meetings, uncomfortable conversations, blind dates) - Pranking friends with a "surprise call" from a celebrity or mutual acquaintance - Fun with friends at parties and gatherings - Practicing call handling or demonstrating apps 🔒 Your Privacy, Respected FakeCall is designed with privacy in mind: • No sign-up or account required • Your settings stay on your device only • Does not read your full contacts book — uses the system contact picker • No phone dialing — no actual calls are made ⚠️ Disclaimer FakeCall is intended for entertainment purposes only. It does not make real phone calls, send messages, or connect to any real phone network. Please use it responsibly and do not use it to harass, scam, or deceive others.
