About this app

QR Scanner: Scan, Create & Share Codes Effortlessly 🔍 Powerful QR & Barcode Scanner Instantly scan any QR code or barcode! Open websites, sms, view product details, check event info, or decode contact cards — all with a single tap. Works flawlessly in low light and handles damaged codes smartly. ✨ Create Custom QR Codes Generate stylish QR codes for URLs, text, contacts, events, or messages. Personalize with colors and save/share your creations instantly. You control the content you create. 📚 Organized Scan History Never lose a scan! Your decoded results are saved locally in a searchable history. Revisit links, contacts, or info anytime. Clear your history easily in settings. 📤 Quick Social Sharing Found something great? Share scanned results or your custom QR codes directly to messaging apps, email, or social media with one tap. 🛡 Simple & Respectful We prioritize your privacy: - Scans happen in real-time; no unwanted data uploads. - History is stored locally on your device. - Optional permissions (Camera, Storage for saving images) are clearly explained. 🎯 Key Features: - QR & barcode scanning - QR code generation (text, URL, contact, sms + more) - Local scan history with search & export - One-tap sharing to social apps/messages - Works offline (scanning & history) - User-friendly, minimalist interface 📎 Perfect for: Opening links • Checking prices • Sharing contacts • Saving promotions • Creating event invites • Storing info securely • And much more! ➡️ Get started now — simplify your digital life!
